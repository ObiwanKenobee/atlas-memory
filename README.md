Experience Principles

Community-first — communities remain custodians of their knowledge.

Consent-first — cultural participation and AI processing require explicit governance.

Context-preserving — knowledge should remain connected to language, place, lineage, and provenance.

Regeneration over extraction — the system should create value for knowledge-holding communities.

Human oversight — AI supports cultural custodians rather than replacing them.

Evidence-aware — documented evidence, oral testimony, interpretation, and machine inference remain distinguishable.

Sovereignty by design — sensitive knowledge can remain private, restricted, or community-controlled.

Governance & Safety Principles

Cultural Sovereignty

Communities must be able to determine how their knowledge is represented, accessed, licensed, and reused.

Consent

Consent should be explicit, contextual, revocable where appropriate, and recorded as a durable governance object.

Sacred Knowledge Protection

Certain knowledge may require restricted access, non-public indexing, geographic obfuscation, or complete exclusion from AI processing.

Benefit Sharing

Commercial or institutional use of community-held knowledge should include appropriate benefit-sharing mechanisms defined with the relevant custodians.

No Cultural Flattening

Do not treat large cultural groups as homogeneous datasets. The system should support local variation, contested interpretations, lineage, dialects, and historical change.

No Unverified AI Claims

AI should not invent traditions, attribute beliefs without evidence, or convert cultural hypotheses into authoritative facts.

Integration Surface

The command center is designed to become a frontend surface over the wider Atlas Sanctum ecosystem.

Potential integration layers include:

Atlas Observatory — cultural risk and preservation intelligence

Atlas Intelligence — AI agents and decision support

Atlas Data — spatial, historical, environmental, and community data

Atlas Impact — regeneration metrics and reporting

Atlas Opportunity — grants, creators, projects, and investment opportunities

Atlas Governance — identity, consent, permissions, and auditability

Atlas Research — publications, datasets, methods, and evidence

The long-term objective is a unified system where cultural intelligence can interact with the rest of Atlas Sanctum without losing its distinct governance requirements.

Non-Goals for the MVP

To prevent scope explosion, the initial release should not attempt to:

Digitize every culture simultaneously

Publicly expose all sacred knowledge

Automatically determine what a community believes

Replace cultural custodians with AI

Create a universal cultural value score

Commercialize cultural assets without governance

Build a global heritage marketplace before validating preservation workflows

The MVP should prove one essential proposition:

Cultural memory can be preserved, governed, explored, and connected to regenerative action through a modern operational interface.

Definition of Success

The MVP succeeds when an authorized community or institution can take a real cultural asset from:

Memory → Preservation → Governance → Intelligence → Action → Regeneration

with every step traceable to people, places, evidence, consent, and outcomes.

Strategic Positioning

The deeper proposition is:

A planetary civilizational memory and regeneration layer that helps living communities preserve identity, transmit wisdom, steward place, and participate in future value creation on terms they can govern.
